IN SHORT
- A decision-support tool for the duty officer at RCB (Poland's Government Centre for Security), not another citizen app. The AI recommends; a human decides.
- A real test: 8 of 9 actual RCB alerts (2020–2026) don't say how long the threat lasts.
- Alerts are scored by fixed rules, so the same text always gets the same score. Demo data is fictional and sending is simulated.

2-MINUTE DEMO (the interface is in Polish; the TRYB DEMO bar at the bottom always shows the next step)
1. ▶ Start demo (bottom bar). If someone has already tested it, click Reset demo. Press P to show or hide the bar.
2. Zdarzenia (Events) → EVT-2026-1042 (heavy rain / flooding): reports from 3 services merged into one event; a rumour is flagged as unverified.
3. Przygotuj projekt alertu (prepare draft) → Analizuj (analyse): 78/100 (no end time, vague instruction).
4. Zastosuj rekomendację (apply recommendation): 92/100.
5. Przekaż do zatwierdzenia (send for approval) → tick the box → Zatwierdź i symuluj wysyłkę (approve and simulate). Shows delivery times for operators A, B and C.
6. Następny etap scenariusza ⏭ (next stage): the situation changes, coverage drops to 83%, SIGNAL recommends an update. The Mapa (Map) menu shows the missed county.
